## MD&A: Revenue Discussion

Total net sales grew modestly year over year, driven by Services revenue growth that
outpaced Products revenue. iPhone revenue was roughly flat, reflecting a maturing
upgrade cycle in key markets and a stronger U.S. dollar that pressured international
results. Services revenue, including the App Store, AppleCare, and subscription
offerings, continued to grow at a double-digit rate and now represents a growing share
of total revenue, contributing to gross margin expansion because Services carry
meaningfully higher margins than hardware products.

## MD&A: Margin Discussion

Gross margin improved year over year, primarily reflecting a favorable mix shift toward
Services and cost efficiencies in the Products segment, partially offset by foreign
exchange headwinds. Management noted that component costs remain a risk given
concentration in a small number of suppliers for critical parts such as advanced
silicon and display panels.

## Risk Factors: Supply Chain Concentration

The Company depends on a small number of suppliers, several of which are single-source,
for certain custom components. Substantially all of the Company's manufacturing is
performed by a small number of contract manufacturers concentrated in China and other
parts of Asia. Disruption of relationships with these suppliers or manufacturers,
including from geopolitical tension, natural disasters, or public health crises, could
materially adversely affect the Company's business, financial condition, and results
of operations.

## Risk Factors: Competitive Pressure

The markets in which the Company competes are characterized by aggressive price
competition, frequent product introductions, evolving industry standards, and price
sensitivity on the part of consumers and businesses. New entrants and existing
competitors have introduced competing products at lower price points, which could
compress margins on the Company's Products segment over time if the Company is unable
to continue differentiating through Services and ecosystem lock-in.

## Risk Factors: Regulatory Scrutiny

The Company is subject to increasing regulatory scrutiny in the United States, European
Union, and other jurisdictions related to App Store practices, antitrust matters, and
digital markets regulation. Adverse rulings or new legislation, such as the EU Digital
Markets Act, could require changes to App Store business practices, including
alternative payment mechanisms and third-party app distribution, which could adversely
affect Services revenue growth and gross margin.
